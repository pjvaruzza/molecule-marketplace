**Date:** 2026-06-24
**Purpose:** US industrial-decarbonization companies that are *beyond seed* and *scaling*, with process inputs/outputs mapped, scored for whether a green-molecule marketplace can help them scale — as a **buyer** of their byproduct (offtake) or **supplier** of their feedstock — and named commercial/BD leads to approach.
**Status:** Funding/status **verified via live web research, June 2026** (sources in section 5). Earlier draft figures corrected.

---

## 0. The framing that matters

Your live repo sells *distribution* of green/byproduct H₂ into the mispriced 50–300 kg/day heat-treat gap. The companies below operate at **kilotonnes/year** — a different scale. So "can the marketplace help them?" splits three ways:

1. **They are SUPPLY for you** — byproduct H₂ or O₂ that needs a buyer.
2. **They are DEMAND you could clear** — they *consume* CO₂ or green H₂ and are cost-bottlenecked on it.
3. **Two-sided matches** — one company's output is another's input (DAC CO₂ → CO₂-to-fuels). Highest-value: clear *both sides of one molecule*.

The strategic question this list forces: **does the marketplace stay H₂-only, or broaden to CO₂/O₂/minerals?** After verification, the **CO₂ two-sided trade is the strongest live opportunity** and the **byproduct-O₂ supply pool is real and unworked** — the pure byproduct-H₂ supply story weakened when Modern Hydrogen died.

## 4. Caveats

- **Policy risk is the dominant variable.** The 2025 DOE OCED clawback (~$3.7B) directly maimed Sublime and Brimstone and is reshaping the sector. Privately-capitalized, offtake-backed firms (Twelve, Infinium, Electra, Boston Metal) are the durable targets.
- **Scale mismatch with the live repo:** these run at kt/yr, not the 50–300 kg/day band. Helping them means broadening the marketplace to CO₂/O₂/minerals **or** treating any H₂ producers as supply into your existing H₂ demand network.
- **O₂ and bulk minerals are low-$/ton** — marketplace economics there hinge on logistics density, the same lesson as the H₂ tube-trailer dwell problem.
- Contact titles are from public team pages + directories (TheOrg, RocketReach) and press; treat directory-sourced titles as ~90% and confirm on the company site before sending.
