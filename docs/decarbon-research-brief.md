# Research Brief: Can the Green-Molecule Marketplace Help Industrial Decarb Startups Scale?

**Date:** 2026-06-24
**Prepared for:** internal strategy / cofounder review
**Companion:** `decarbon-industrials-market-map.md` (full data tables, all contacts, source URLs)

---

## Executive summary

We screened **~20 US industrial-decarbonization companies** that are beyond seed and actively scaling, mapped each one's process **inputs and outputs**, and asked a single question: *does a green-molecule marketplace clear a transaction this company needs to hit scale?*

**The finding:** the answer is yes for **7 of them** — but not the way our live repo assumes. Our hydrogen-distribution thesis (the 50–300 kg/day heat-treat gap) doesn't touch these companies; they run at kilotonnes/year. What *does* connect is the layer underneath: every one of the seven either **produces a byproduct molecule that needs an offtaker** or **consumes a feedstock molecule it's cost-bottlenecked on.** That's the same market-making muscle, applied one altitude up.

The strongest single opportunity is a **two-sided CO₂ trade** — direct-air-capture supply (Heirloom) matched to CO₂-to-fuels demand (Twelve, Infinium, Air Company) — where all parties survived the 2025 funding shakeout and all are cost-bottlenecked on exactly the transaction we'd intermediate.

**The strategic fork this forces:** to help these companies, the marketplace has to broaden from H₂-only to a **CO₂ / O₂ / H₂ exchange.** That's a real product decision, not a tweak. This brief lays out the evidence; the decision is ours to make.

## What we did

Four parallel research passes across cement, carbon removal/CO₂-utilization, clean hydrogen/green metals, and green chemicals/heat — then a second verification pass (June 2026) on every shortlisted company to confirm it's still alive, confirm current funding, and pull named commercial leads. Funding and survival status are current as of June 2026; full sources in the companion doc.

## What we learned

**1. The sector just went through a culling — screen for survivors, not logos.**
The 2025 DOE OCED clawback (~$3.7B across 24 projects) maimed several marquee names. **Modern Hydrogen wound down entirely** (Oct 2025) — which stings, because it was our closest pure-fit (byproduct H₂ + solid carbon). **Sublime Systems lost its $87M grant and cut two-thirds of staff.** **CarbonCapture Inc** fled to Canada at 1/500th its original scale. The durable targets are the **privately-capitalized, offtake-backed** firms: Twelve (TPG, $645M), Infinium (Brookfield, $1.1B), Electra (Capricorn + Temasek, $186M), Boston Metal (Tata, fresh $75M).

**2. The molecule fit is real — and it's bigger than hydrogen.**
- **CO₂ is the strongest trade.** Supply (Heirloom DAC) and demand (Twelve, Infinium, Air Company eFuels) both exist, both alive, both bottlenecked.
- **Byproduct O₂ is an unworked pool.** Electra and Boston Metal vent pure oxygen from electrolytic metal-making at scale. Nobody is clearing it.
- **Byproduct H₂ weakened** when Modern Hydrogen died — but supply still exists (Electric Hydrogen, Koloma) against real demand (Infinium, Air Company, green ammonia).
- **Byproduct minerals** — Brimstone throws off smelter-grade alumina alongside cement; niche but a clean offtake-matching job.

**3. The warmest door speaks our language.**
Twelve's CCO, **Ram Ramprasad, is ex-Linde** — he ran commercial at a global industrial-gas major. He already thinks in the molecule-distribution terms our marketplace is built on. Best first conversation to pressure-test the whole thesis.

## Recommendation

1. **Take one Tier-1 conversation before committing to scope** — Ram Ramprasad at Twelve. Use it to test whether a molecule-clearing layer is something these buyers would actually pay for.
2. **Decide the scope fork deliberately:** H₂-only (smaller, focused, matches the repo) vs. CO₂/O₂/H₂ exchange (where the live demand actually is). The evidence leans toward broadening, but it's a real bet.
3. **If we broaden, lead with the CO₂ two-sided trade** (Heirloom ↔ Twelve/Infinium/Air Company) — it's the cleanest proof that we can clear both ends of one molecule.

## Caveats

- These run at kt/yr, not the 50–300 kg/day band our model simulates. "Helping them" means either broadening the marketplace or treating H₂ producers as supply into our existing network.
- O₂ and bulk minerals are low-$/ton; marketplace economics there hinge on logistics density — the same dwell-cost lesson as the H₂ tube-trailer math.
- Policy risk is the dominant variable in this sector right now. Re-verify any company's status before it enters an investor doc or an outbound email.
