Why this wins

De-risked by design

Supply is locked before buyers are pursued, and back-to-back pricing means we never carry commodity risk. The business proves itself transaction by transaction rather than betting capital on an unvalidated thesis.

A beachhead, then a template

We win California first — its LCFS + 45V credit stack is the richest in the country — then replicate the playbook region by region. Concentrated liquidity in one market beats thin presence in five.

A moat the majors won't contest

Air Products and Linde have no structural incentive to serve 10–100 kg/day buyers. We own the long tail through operational trust — on-time, on-spec delivery — which compounds into switching costs they can't easily attack.

Margin where others see none

The durable margin isn't trucking coordination — it's monetizing LCFS and 45V credits for producers too small to do it alone. Set the credit fee to zero in the model and watch how thin a pure-logistics business would be.
