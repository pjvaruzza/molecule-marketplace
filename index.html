The thesis in one screen

Supply-first

Lock soft supply commitments from a handful of producers before approaching buyers. You can't sell what you don't have, and a real offer makes every buyer call credible.

Geography-first

Win one region before expanding. California's credit stack (LCFS + 45V) makes the green-molecule story pencil where pure logistics spread alone would be too thin.

Manual-first

Execute the first deals by hand. The operational knowledge from coordinating real deliveries can't be replicated by building software first. Platform is a Year 2 investment.

Credit margin is the moat

The durable margin isn't trucking coordination — it's orchestrating credit monetization for small players the majors aren't structured to serve. Watch the model with the credit fee at zero.
